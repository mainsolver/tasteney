## Brand & Style
The design system embodies the ritual, refinement, and tactile prestige of an artisanal tasting room. It caters to sommeliers, cicerones, master roasters, and discerning enthusiasts tracking vintages, single-origin roasts, botanical distillations, and rare harvests.

The aesthetic marries **Tactile Luxury Editorial** with **Soft Glassmorphism**. Layouts feel like a private curatorial journal bound in rich textures: expansive ivory margins, disciplined typographic scale, luminous translucent panels, and hairline metallic accents. The interface avoids cold utility in favor of warmth, quiet authority, and visceral sensory engagement.

## Colors
The palette evokes aged oak barrels, deep vintage pours, and fine crystal stemware:

- **Primary (`#6b1724`)**: Deep Burgundy. Represents body, maceration, and rich vinous heritage. Used for hero titles, critical calls to action, and focal brand expressions.
- **Secondary (`#c49a45` / Pale `#eedcba`)**: Champagne Gold. Expresses effervescence, patina, and cellar lighting. Used for active states, key ratings, sensory chart fills, and subtle metallic borders.
- **Tertiary (`#2e3b32`)**: Deep Forest/Botanical Slate. Anchors herbal, tea, and botanical profiles; used for secondary contextual tags and terroir accents.
- **Neutral (`#fcf9f4`)**: Soft Ivory Cream. Serves as the parchment ground, eliminating clinical digital glare and enhancing the warmth of photography. Surface variants transition to a slightly deeper warm linen (`#f4eee5`) for layered cards.
- **Surface & Glass Tinting**: Frosted overlays rely on white or ivory washes (`rgba(252, 249, 244, 0.72)`) mixed with micro-borders tinted in translucent champagne gold (`rgba(196, 154, 69, 0.25)`).

## Typography
Typographic rhythm sets an intellectual, editorial pacing. 

- **Headlines & Editorial Quotations**: `Newsreader` delivers literary gravity and classical proportion, evoking sommelier compendiums and printed tasting gazettes. The italic treatment of `body-lg` is reserved for tasting impressions, sensory summaries, and winemaker notes.
- **Interface & Data Density**: `Manrope` handles structured data, cellar metrics, ABV percentages, extraction yields, and harvest dates. Its geometric clarity keeps technical cellar management legible at small scales without competing with the serif titles.
- **Labels**: Rendered uppercase in `Manrope` with generous letter spacing to replicate blind-embossed stationery and high-end spirit labels.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid 12-column grid on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single-column structured scroll on mobile. 

Generous outer margins create an open, quiet breathing room reminiscent of luxury art books. Content does not crowd the viewport; structural containers float within intentional negative space. 

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Journal views use asymmetric 7:5 splits (visual showcase and sensory wheels on the left, chronological cellar logs and metrics on the right).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: Single column with `1.25rem` outer margins. Sensory charts and bottle metadata stack cleanly with vertical step increments driven by `space-lg`.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth relies on **Tonal Warm Glassmorphism** paired with diffuse ambient shadows. The interface avoids aggressive drop shadows in favor of ambient cellar light and translucent materials.

- **Base Canvas**: Solid neutral cream (`#fcf9f4`) with subtle micro-grain texture.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Containers & Logs)**: Off-ivory fill (`#f5efe6`) with an ambient drop shadow tinted in deep burgundy: `0 8px 30px rgba(92, 19, 31, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 2 (Glassmorphic Beverage Cards)**: Backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with an ivory substrate (`rgba(252, 249, 244, 0.75)`), framed by a 1px border of `rgba(196, 154, 69, 0.3)`. Shadow: `0 12px 36px rgba(107, 23, 36, 0.07)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals, Overlays, Sensory Zoom)**: Elevated sheets use `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)` over `rgba(252, 249, 244, 0.88)` with a 1px champagne gold rim (`#c49a45`), elevated by `0 24px 60px rgba(92, 19, 31, 0.12)`.

## Shapes
The design system embraces an elongated, organic pill shape language. 

- **Buttons, Metadata Tags, and Interactive Filters**: Fully rounded pill silhouettes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) provide smooth, polished contact points that recall smoothed beach stones, cork seals, and wax stamps.
- **Cards and Display Surfaces**: Employ softened structural radii (`rounded-2xl` / `1.5rem` to `2rem`), striking an intentional contrast against the hyper-rounded pill chips nestled inside them.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Deep burgundy fill (`#6b1724`), champagne gold text or crisp white, fully pill-shaped. Hover introduces an ambient gold halo (`0 0 16px rgba(196, 154, 69, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary (Cellar Outline)**: Transparent ground, 1px champagne border (`#c49a45`), burgundy text (`#6b1724`). Hover fills with soft gold tint (`rgba(238, 220, 186, 0.25)`).
- **Tertiary / Text**: Manrope uppercase tracking, subtle champagne underline on hover.

### Chips & Pill Tags
- **Sensory & Varietal Pills**: Height 28px, pill-shaped, ivory glass fill with a 1px soft metallic border (`rgba(196, 154, 69, 0.4)`).
- **Active / Selected State**: Burgundy background (`#6b1724`) with ivory typography.

### Beverage Cards (Glassmorphic)
- Built on translucent layered glass (`rgba(252, 249, 244, 0.7)`), 1px gold hairline border, pill tags floating in the header.
- Content includes high-contrast bottle/pour imagery, producer, vintage/harvest year, and an inline sensory summary badge.

### Sensory Radar Chart
- Spider/radar diagram representing flavor axes (e.g., Acidity, Tannin, Sweetness, Body, Minerality, Oak / Roast Level).
- Axes lines drawn in pale champagne (`#eedcba`). Radar area rendered in deep burgundy at 35% opacity with a solid 1.5px burgundy boundary and gold anchor nodes.

### Vintage & Roast Metric Bars
- Horizontal micro-meters tracking drink windows ("Drink Now", "Hold", "Peak 2028-2032") and coffee roast development (Light City to French).
- Base track: Warm cream-gray (`#e8dfd3`). Fill: Gradient from champagne gold (`#c49a45`) to deep burgundy (`#6b1724`).

### Form Inputs & Tasting Log Fields
- Clean underline or pill-framed input containers. Text fields rest on translucent ivory with focused borders transitioning into rich burgundy. Placeholder text set in italicized `Newsreader` to prompt evocative notes.

### Lists & Inventory Logs
- Table rows separated by hairline champagne dividers (`rgba(196, 154, 69, 0.2)`). Columns balance classical Newsreader labels with monospace or high-legibility Manrope figures for bin locations, bottle counts, and purchase prices.
